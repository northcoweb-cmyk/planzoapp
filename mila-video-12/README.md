## Music: "1997 (Extended Mix)" (2:18.21 to 2:38.20)
- **Tempo:** 132.0 BPM, one beat = 0.45445 s. Fitted to individual kick onsets in three sections, and the main drop's first kick (2:25.48) sits exactly 256 beats after the 0:29.14 kick.
- **Drop:** the main drop is song beat 320, which is video beat 16. The song's own build runs untouched underneath the to-do montage.
- **Reverb hang:** on b15.5 the dry song cuts out and the half-beat before it hangs in a 4.5 s hall under the "send", then the drop slams in.
- **Sound effects:** sub hits on the words, shutter clicks on the photo strobe and grid, whips on the cuts, a stamp, pops, the approve chime. The end reverb tail is wrapped to the start.
