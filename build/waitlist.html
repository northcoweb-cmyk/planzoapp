How it works

One link. Everybody's in.

1

Say what you want to do

“Beach Saturday with 6 friends.” “Something free tonight.” No forms, no setup.

2

Drop the link in your group chat

Your friends tap it and answer a few quick questions in their browser. No download. No account. No password.

3

Planzo narrows it down as answers come in

Once five people say pizza, nobody else gets asked “pizza or burgers?” — and the one vegetarian still gets somewhere they can eat.

4

You get an actual plan

Times, real places, how you're getting there, the weather, and what it'll cost each of you.

It doesn't guess.

Planzo won't invent a restaurant, a price, or a showtime to look clever. Every place, time and cost comes from a real source — and when something can't be verified, it says so instead of making it up. Estimates are labelled as estimates.

Questions

Do my friends need to download anything?

No. They tap your link, type their name, and answer a few questions in whatever browser they already have open. Only the person creating plans needs an account.

Is it free?

Yes. The free version is a real product, not a demo — planning, shareable links, group decisions and local recommendations are all included. Planzo Pro will be $4.99/month for bigger groups and multi-day trips.

Is this just ChatGPT with extra steps?

No. Planzo collects everyone's actual constraints — budget, availability, how far they'll travel, what they can't eat — and resolves them against real places, weather and transport. A language model helps with the words. It is never the source of a fact.

What do you do with my email?

We email you once when Planzo opens. We don't sell it, and we don't add you to anything else.
